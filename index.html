Pricing questions

Frequently asked questions

What is included in the initial price?

It covers only the development or pilot scope agreed in your proposal. For the Testing System managed plan, the listed initial amount is ₹30,000 development plus ₹5,000 setup (₹35,000 total), before hosting/domain, third-party services or customizations unless explicitly included.

Is there a recurring maintenance charge?

Yes for the managed offers: ₹800–₹1,200/month for a basic website, ₹2,000–₹3,000/month for a limited-scope web application/LMS, and ₹3,500/month for the Testing System. The exact support and maintenance scope should be written into the proposal.

Are hosting and domain costs included?

They are not included by default in the indicative prices shown here. Any hosting, domain and third-party service charges should be listed separately in the proposal.

Can the product be customized?

Requirements can be discussed and scoped. Additional features, integrations, pages and customizations may change the price and will be quoted before work begins.

What is the difference between managed deployment and ownership?

With a managed plan, FalconLab manages the agreed deployment and paid maintenance scope. The ₹95,000 ownership listing is provisional and does not define transfer deliverables; confirm source-code, database, documentation, handover and ongoing costs in writing.

Can an institute start with a pilot?

Yes. A paid pilot is indicatively ₹2,000–₹4,000 one time. The scope, student limit, duration, setup assistance and included features will be specified in a proposal before the pilot starts.

How are additional features and support requests priced?

They are assessed against the agreed scope and quoted separately. Ask for the requested work, support coverage and any additional charges to be documented before approval.
